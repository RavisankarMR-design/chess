Card counting (Hi-Lo)

Count hidden. Track it yourself, then tick the box to check.
How it works

2-6 = +1, 7-9 = 0, 10-A = -1. True count = running count / decks left. Positive = more tens and aces left = good for you.

Quick rules

6 decks · dealer stands on all 17 · blackjack pays 3:2 · double any two cards · split once · no surrender.
Dealer 2-6 is weak (may bust). 7-A is strong. Always split A and 8. Never split 10s.
